Questions

Straight answers

Do I have to buy something to enter?
No. The free entry form above puts 10 entries in the same pool as paid entries, one per person per day, and the Official Rules include a mail-in route too. A purchase never changes the weight of a single entry — it only changes how many you hold.
How are entries counted?
Baseline is 1 entry per $1 spent before multipliers. Giveaway #001 runs a 10X launch multiplier, poster packs pay a bonus rate shown on each pack, and VIP members multiply the entries on their orders by their tier (5X, 10X, or 25X).
When does Giveaway #001 end?
Official start and end dates are published in the Official Rules before entries open — the countdown at the top of this preview is a placeholder clock until then. Once the store is live, that countdown is the real one.
How is the winner picked?
After the window closes, an independent sweepstakes administrator draws one entry at random from the full pool. The winner is contacted, verified for eligibility, and announced on the Winners wall.
What exactly does the winner get?
The 2025 Toyota Tacoma TRD Off-Road featured on this page, transferred with its title. The truck's color and cab/bed configuration are listed in the specs section above — the winner gets the truck exactly as described there.
Who pays the taxes on the truck?
The winner is responsible for income taxes on the prize value, as the Official Rules spell out. Vehicle prizes are reported as required by law; read the rules section before entering.
What are digital poster packs?
Downloadable high-resolution posters of the giveaway Tacoma, delivered instantly — no shipping, no waiting. Each pack also carries the best entry rate in the store.
When will I be charged?
This page is a preview — the store isn't open yet. Checkout goes live when JUSTRUCKS moves to Shopify, and nothing here can charge you today.
